FAQ

Frequently asked.

Which platforms do you support?+

Spotify and YouTube Music today. Apple Music, Tidal and Deezer are on the roadmap.

Is my data secure?+

Yes. We use OAuth and never store your listening data. You can revoke access from your dashboard anytime.

How accurate is matching?+

Over 95%. The rest land in the Missing Tracks view, where you can confirm or skip.

Can I sync both directions?+

Yes. Pick any source and destination. Continuous two-way sync is opt-in per playlist.
